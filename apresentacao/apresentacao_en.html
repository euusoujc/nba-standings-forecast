VALIDATION

Seasons are the unit of honesty

  1. 01

    Leave-one-season-out

    Train on 15 seasons, test on the held-out one; 16 folds.

  2. 02

    Temporal holdout

    Train 2010-22, test 2023-26: does it age well?

  3. 03

    Nested α search

    Inner GroupKFold by season; one-standard-error rule picks α.

  4. 04

    Two metrics

    MAE in wins (82-game scale) and Spearman of conference ranks.

LOOKING BACK

What worked, what we'd change

Worked

  • Season-grouped CV, no leakage
  • Simple linear model beat forests
  • Backtesting the simulation, not trusting it
  • Gain is largest early: 13.3 → 7.2 at N = 5

Next time

  • Model injuries, trades, roster changes
  • Update ratings game by game
  • Official NBA tiebreakers, not random
  • Player-level priors (e.g. minutes-weighted)
