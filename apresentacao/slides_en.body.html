CORE IDEA

Early records regress

Each point above .500 after 15 games is worth only 63% by the end of the season.

WHAT WE TESTED

Two baselines, four models

BASELINE

Keep win %

Today's record holds until the end.

BASELINE

Pythagorean

LINEAR

OLS

Least squares on six standardized features.

L2 · CHOSEN

Ridge

Shrinks coefficients and keeps the point-based features, which move together, in check.

L1

Lasso

Can zero out redundant features.

TREES

Random Forest

Nonlinearity check: 500 trees, min leaf 10.

06 / 14
VALIDATION

Leave a whole season out each time

  1. 01

    Leave-one-season-out

    Train on 15 seasons, test on the one left out; 16 rounds.

  2. 02

    Temporal holdout

    Train 2010-22, test 2023-26. Does it hold up in recent years?

  3. 03

    Nested α search

    Inner CV also by season; the 1-standard-error rule picks α.

  4. 04

    Two metrics

    Error in wins (82-game scale) and Spearman of the standings.

WHY RIDGE

Same error, coefficients that make sense

Point diff and net rating correlate at 0.999. OLS gives one +0.16 and the other −0.18. Ridge splits the weight.

LOOKING BACK

What worked, what we'd change

Worked

  • Validating by season, no leakage
  • A simple linear model tied the forest
  • Testing the simulation on the past before trusting it
  • With 5 games the error already drops from 13.3 to 7.2

Next time

  • Model injuries, trades and roster changes
  • Keep going with 20, 25, 30-game versions
  • Update ratings after every game
  • Official NBA tiebreakers
